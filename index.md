# Projects, Progress, and the Occasional Rabbit Hole

By Michael Coar

I enjoy figuring out how things work—and occasionally discovering
that a “small upgrade” has become an entirely new project.

This journal follows what I am building, what I am learning, and
what I would do differently next time.

## Home Network Security and Automation

This three-part series follows my journey from wondering what
my devices were doing online to rebuilding my network and moving
home automation closer to home.

Start with Entry 1 and follow the project through Entries 2 and 3.

## What Comes Next?

I will add more entries as these projects develop, including
updates, discoveries, and the occasional detour into AI,
home-lab equipment, and making things.

But next up are a couple new servers

One running Voice to Text and Text to Voice on a computer
running a 3090, so I have 24GB of VRAM, which should be
plenty for sufficient models. 
Looking at Voicebox to do that. 
Again, keeping with my theme of running as much locally
(and off the cloud) as possible. 

My goal is to connect that to my Home Assistant server

Then after that I splurged on a Blackwell card 
(before prices went nuts)
And plan to run an LLM on it, with controlled access to
the Internet, as well as links to something like ChatGPT
when the project is too big for it to handle.
